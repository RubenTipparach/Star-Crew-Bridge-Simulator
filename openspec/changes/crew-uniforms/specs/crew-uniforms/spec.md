# crew-uniforms

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every crew member wears their division's colour

Every crew member, player or NPC, SHALL wear the tunic of their division: operations gold, science blue,
engineering red or support green, by their department aboard or the station they hold on the bridge.

#### Scenario: The drill's bridge
- **WHEN** a crew holds Helm, Science and Engineering in the drill
- **THEN** the helmsman wears gold, the science officer blue and the engineer red

#### Scenario: A medic in the walk
- **WHEN** a medical NPC walks the decks
- **THEN** it wears science blue
