# Proposal: power-routing

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-11, on the power grid mockup's floor plan: "A power grid needs to connect everything together",
"Conduits are placed in breaker boxes throughout the ship", then "Conduit boxes can be turned on or off to route power
through the ship. Too many conduits open can cause the ship to shut down temporarily and reset the configuration to
factory settings".

Today the grid has 14 nodes: four switchboard sections, the emergency bus and nine distribution panels, most of them
on decks B and C. A load hangs off a panel with nothing drawn between them, so on the plan most loads are loose dots,
and a room with no panel in it is fed from a panel two rooms or a deck away. Engineering can set a group's power and
open a group's feed breaker, but it cannot route power through the ship.

## What changes

- **A conduit box in every room**, generated from the layout: the room's existing switchboard or panel where it has
  one, otherwise a box on the wall beside the door its feed comes in by.
- **Conduits run box to box through the doorways** (one a doorway, beside the existing trunks and ties), and every load
  is wired to its room's box by a feeder drawn on the plan. Each box lights its own room.
- **Routing**: a box can be switched off (its room and everything fed only through it go dark), and every conduit can
  be closed or opened at its box. The factory configuration closes the trunks, the ties and one feed into each room;
  the other doorway runs are spare routes, open, for rerouting around damage.
- **The trip**: every live run (closed, whole, both boxes on) adds to the load on the main protection. More live runs
  than it allows (`power.json` `routing`) trips the grid: everything past the switchboards goes dark for a few seconds,
  then the configuration returns to its factory settings and the grid comes back.
- The power grid mockup shows the boxes and feeders, takes a click on a box or a conduit to switch it, counts the live
  runs against the limit and shows a trip.

## Impact

- Data: `data/ships/tern/power.json`: 20 new boxes, a conduit a doorway with its factory state, a feeder a load, the
  lighting panels by box, a `routing` block (the limit, the dark time) and a `distribution` block (the generator's
  rules). Generated by `tools/power/boxes.py` from `layout.json` and the hand-authored part of `power.json`.
- Mockups: `docs/mockups/lib/shipsystems.js` (box and conduit switches, the live count, the trip and the reset) and
  `docs/mockups/power-grid.html`.
- Code: `sc-core::power` reads the new fields and starts every run at its factory state. Switching from the engine's
  Engineering console, and the trip, wait for the owner's approval of the mockup (CLAUDE.md 10).
- Changes the fight: more conduits for hits to cut, and rooms on one feed until a spare is closed.
