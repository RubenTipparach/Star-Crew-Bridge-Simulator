# Hound enemy ship concept studies

The owner requested three enemy ship designs, each developed in order: floor plan,
hull, then a floor plan adjusted to that hull. Rough textures and an HTML review
come before production assets.

Base the studies on main at 7653f7a, including the Hound and Jackal enemy work.
The current Hound combat layout has seven damage compartments but no walkable
deck plan. Its 30 m damage hull, earlier 45 m mockup and engine silhouette are
separate provisional representations. These concepts explore a consistent ship
with real crew space and two 8 m Jackal fighters.

Present three Hound directions: Pursuer, a narrow raider; Splitjaw, a forked
carrier; and Bulwark, a broad armored corvette. Each keeps the Hound's bridge,
magazine, port and starboard laser rooms, reactor, hangar and drive, adding actual
circulation and crew support. These are review alternatives, not three new
runtime enemy classes.

Deliver one interactive HTML review with three selectable sets, orbitable hulls,
deck plans, cutaways, room information, human scale and reviewed captures.
The live Hound combat data and game behavior are not changed by this study.
