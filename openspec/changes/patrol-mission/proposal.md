# Proposal: patrol-mission

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-14: "have the ship undock from star base, go patrol a star system and encounter hostiles", and "the
view through the window should reflect whats going on around the ship". Then 2026-10-15: "there should b something
describing a patrol mission where we travel to various planets in the star system and encounter enemies we have to
clear out, begining of the game starts at space dock", "we do start at the oneil cylinders btw", and "we should have
some baked lighting in the interior hanger bay".

As built (phase 2) the patrol undocks from the O'Neill pair, jumps to an empty beacon and to one fight, and goes home.
The owner's 2026-10-15 words make it a tour of the system's planets with a fight to win at each (phase 3).

Today the only mission is the drill (`coop-drill`): the Tern starts in open space at the Shoals at 60 m/s, the Hound
appears 6 km ahead, and the fight is the whole game. Nothing happens before it and nothing after it but the debrief.
The star system it happens in exists only as a sun, a planet and a star field painted on the sky by
`data/space/exterior.json`, the relay the helm's COURSE flies to is a point with nothing drawn at it, and the
starbase the owner names does not exist at all.

The windows already show more than they did (`coop-drill` 9.1, `tern-exterior`): the sky turned by the Tern's
attitude, the Hound, its fighters, bolts, missiles and bursts at the back of the depth buffer, the Tern's own hull. But
the hull is lit by a sun baked into its vertex colours from a direction 25 degrees off the sky's sun, and that light
stays fixed to the ship while the sky turns round it in a fight; the Hound and its fighters are lit by two near copies
of one rule; and nothing outside changes with where the Tern is, so a mission that travels has nothing to show for it.

## What Changes

- **A star system as data** (design 2): `data/space/systems/shoals.json`, the Shoals system: its star, three planets,
  a moon, the starbase Tamsin Station, the relay at the Shoals and the points of interest a patrol visits, with their
  positions in the system frame (metres, `f64`) and the arrival point of each. It is `flight-and-navigation` 13's
  system file, kept beside the exterior's other data.
- **The clearing route** (phase 3, design 1, 5, 6, 12b): from the O'Neill pair to three stops, each with hostiles to
  clear before the next jump, in rising strength: **Cinder's ore lanes** (a pack of three Jackals raiding the haulers out
  of Cinder Yards, found by science's sweep), **the Pell beacon at Orrin** (the Hound and its two Jackals: the drill's
  fight), and **the Shoals relay** in the belt (a raider and two Jackals, the hardest). A stop is clear when its group is
  destroyed and a sweep shows nothing; the ship's damage, fires and missiles carry from stop to stop, so the crew put
  the ship right between fights; the debrief says PATROL COMPLETE or names the stops cleared. About 25-30 minutes.
- **The bay's light**: the Tern starts in Tamsin Station's bay lit by baked light (spacedock 8a), and its hull is lit
  by the bay there, not by the star (a fix to what phase 1 built).
- **The patrol mission as built** (design 1, 4-8): `data/missions/patrol-shoals.json`. The crew muster aboard the Tern docked
  at Tamsin Station; engineering walks to the engine room console and brings the reactor and the drive up off shore
  power; the captain orders the undock; helm releases the clamps and backs the Tern out of its berth on the bow
  thrusters along the berth's axis; past the station's 2 km mass lock the drive spools and the Tern jumps
  (`flight-and-navigation` 7's jump drive, chosen over a cruise drive, with its numbers) to the first waypoint, the
  picket beacon at Pell; science sweeps it; the next jump takes the Tern to the relay at the Shoals, where the Hound and
  its fighters are stripping the relay and the drill's fight runs as it does today; with the Hound destroyed or driven
  past 5 km, the Tern jumps home. About 11 minutes for a crew that knows the ship.
- **Every step on foot** (the law): each step above is a console reached by walking to its station, and the bots do
  the same, saying what they do in chat (`crew-chat` owns the chat; this change names their lines, design 9).
- **The starbase, a placeholder until its model arrives** (design 3): a low-poly Tamsin Station generated by the client
  (a spindle, a habitat ring on four spokes, two berth arms, lit windows, beacons), about 1,200 triangles, replaced by
  `assets/models/exteriors/starbase.glb` when the `starbase` change lands. The berth's cradle is the existing dock frame
  of `data/space/exterior.json`.
- **The view through every window from the real exterior state** (design 10, 11): what each window and each viewscreen
  shows in each phase, from the snapshot and the system file, never a canned backdrop: the berth's girders and the
  station's lights while docked and sliding past as the Tern backs out; the star, the nearest planet and the station
  receding on the way out; star streaks in the jump; Pell's gas giant at the beacon; the Hound, its fighters, bolts,
  missiles and bursts, and the relay, at the Shoals. One sun lights the sky, the Tern's hull and every object outside.
- **Built now, in the client's drawing only** (design 12): the Tern's hull relit by the sky's sun as the Tern turns
  (the bake's light divided out in the deck program), one lighting rule for everything outside, and the relay at the
  Shoals drawn where the helm's COURSE flies, from the placeholder station generator the starbase uses too.
- **A mockup** (`docs/mockups/patrol.html`): the Tern docked at the placeholder starbase, undocking, in the jump and
  in the encounter, from outside and through the bridge's port window, with its Pi 5 cost.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `patrol-mission`: the patrol from the starbase across a system to an encounter and home, its phases, its jobs on
  foot and what the bots do.
- `exterior-view`: what the windows and the viewscreens show, from the exterior state the server sends and the system
  file, lit by the system's star.

### Modified Capabilities

None in `openspec/specs/` (it holds no capability yet). `flight-and-navigation` 13's `data/systems/<id>.json` is
placed at `data/space/systems/<id>.json`; `missions` 3's warp out is the same jump this change uses between waypoints.

## Impact

- Phase 1 (this change's write-up, the mockup, and the client's exterior drawing): `openspec/changes/patrol-mission/`,
  `docs/mockups/patrol.html`, `crates/sc-client` (`hull.rs`, `drill_bridge.rs`, a new `outside.rs`, `ships3d.rs`'s
  station generator, `drill.rs`'s objects), `crates/sc-render/shaders/deck.glsl` (the hull's relight), and
  `data/space/exterior.json` (the beacons' blink).
- Phase 3 (the clearing route, design 12b): `sc-core::combat` (`patrol.rs`'s stops and groups, `fighters.rs`'s pack
  with no carrier, the fight re-armed at each stop with the Tern kept, the `clear` objective, the outcome),
  `data/missions/patrol-shoals.json`, `data/space/systems/shoals.json` (Cinder's ore lanes), the bots, the briefing's
  route and the hull's light in the bay (`outside.rs`). The raider's class comes from `missions`.
- Phase 2 (the mission's engine, after `crew-chat`'s protocol work is committed): `sc-core` (the system file's loader,
  the patrol's phases, the berth, undocking, the jump between waypoints, the encounter's start in the system frame),
  `sc-net` (the phase, the spool and the waypoint in the snapshot; the undock, shore power and jump commands),
  `sc-server`, the bots, and the consoles' new controls, each in `docs/mockups/consoles.html` for approval first
  (CLAUDE.md 10).
