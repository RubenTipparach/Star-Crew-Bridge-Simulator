## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The walkways beside the shuttle stay in air through a launch
The two galleries beside the shuttle and the bay control booth SHALL be compartments of their own,
separated from the hangar floor by pressure walls with windows and by pressure doors that stay shut,
with no override, while the hangar is below 80 kPa.

#### Scenario: A launch with crew in the gallery
- **WHEN** the hangar is pumped down and the Petrel launches while two crew stand unsuited in the port gallery
- **THEN** the gallery stays at cabin pressure and they watch the launch through its windows

### Requirement: A launch leaves deck B's route open
The galleries SHALL keep their doors to engineering and the spine open to use during a launch, so the
forward ship and engineering stay joined on deck B.

#### Scenario: Walking aft during a launch
- **WHEN** a crew member walks from the spine to engineering while the hangar is in vacuum
- **THEN** they pass through a gallery without a suit
