# Proposal: fire that spreads across a room, and putting it out

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-08: "I need another js mockup of putting out fires. There should be a fire propagation sim".

`damage-control` section 3 already simulates fire, room by room, in `docs/mockups/lib/shipsystems.js`: a fire's
heat release grows on the t-squared "fast" curve, burns the room's oxygen into carbon dioxide and smoke, heats the
air, spreads to a neighbour through an open door when that room's air passes 300 C, and is cut by extinguishers,
water mist, inert gas and venting. What it cannot do is show a fire, or let anyone fight one: a room's fire is one
number. An extinguisher "cuts the heat release by 60 kW a second", wherever it is pointed. There is no flame to aim
at, no edge to work from, nothing that creeps toward the bunks or flares back up behind you.

## What Changes

- **Fire on the floor** (design 1-3): every room's floor is cut into 0.5 m cells, each with the fuel of what stands
  on it (bedding, furniture, cable trays, machinery, bare deck). A fire is the set of cells burning. A burning cell
  preheats its neighbours until they ignite; the room's hot layer preheats every cell, and at 300 C the whole room
  flashes over. The room's heat release is the sum of its cells, so the room model (oxygen, smoke, heat, damage,
  spread through doors) is unchanged and still the one authority for gases and heat.
- **Calibrated to the table** (design 4): an unattended fire in the quarters still passes 1 MW about 113 s after a
  50 kW seed, and `damage-control`'s measured table is rerun with the cell model by a committed harness.
- **Putting it out** (design 5): an extinguisher is a cone that lands on cells. Its 60 kW a second goes only to the
  burning cells it covers, so aim matters: sweep the base from the near edge. A cell knocked down with fuel left can
  reflash once the agent on it wears off. Mist, inert gas and venting act as before, through the room model.
- **A mockup** (design 6): `docs/mockups/fire.html`, a deck of the Tern in three.js with its own lights: walk in with
  an extinguisher from the damage control locker and fight a fire in first person, or watch the deck from above as
  the damage control board, opening and shutting doors, venting, flooding and setting fires to watch them spread.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `fire-spread`: fire as burning floor cells inside a room, their spread, flashover, and aimed suppression.

### Modified Capabilities

- None in `openspec/specs/` (nothing about fire is built in the engine yet). `damage-control`'s design keeps its
  room model; its section 3 table is regenerated by this change's harness (design 4).

## Impact

- `docs/mockups/lib/firespread.js` (new), the one implementation of the cell model in the mockups;
  `docs/mockups/lib/shipsystems.js` takes a room's heat release from it.
- `docs/mockups/fire.html` (new), `tools/mockups/fire_cases.mjs` (new, a measurement instrument: the table's cases).
- `data/ships/tern/atmosphere.json` `fire.cells`: the cell size, fuel classes and spread constants.
- Later, in the engine: `sc-core::fire`, beside the room atmosphere step. Its Pi 5 cost is design 7.
