## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Separate faction collections
The review SHALL preserve Pursuer, Splitjaw and Bulwark as Federation concepts
and present three newly shaped Grabon concepts in a separate linked collection.

#### Scenario: Browse the earlier studies
- **WHEN** the owner opens Federation concepts
- **THEN** all three earlier hulls, plans and deck cutaways remain available

### Requirement: Grabon silhouette and fitted decks
Each Grabon study SHALL have a distinct command head, narrow habitable connection
and swept machinery wings, with retained first plans and fitted convex rooms.

#### Scenario: Review the sequence
- **WHEN** the owner changes between initial plan, hull and fitted plan
- **THEN** the same study shows its design sequence, aligned portals and connected rooms

### Requirement: Inspectable rough concepts
Each study SHALL provide orbiting hull and deck-cutaway views with simple
textures, human and fighter scale references, normal and red-alert interior
lighting, and actual visible geometry counts within the stated concept budget.

#### Scenario: Open a deck directly
- **WHEN** the owner opens a link naming a ship, fitted stage, deck and cutaway mode
- **THEN** that deck's 3D floor plan is shown and can be orbited and inspected
