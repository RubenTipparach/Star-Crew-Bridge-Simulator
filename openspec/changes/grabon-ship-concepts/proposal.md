# Grabon ship concept studies

The owner identifies Pursuer, Splitjaw and Bulwark as Federation designs. Preserve
those three studies as Federation concepts, with their layouts and cutaways.
Give the Grabons a separate collection inspired by the head, neck, swept wings
and engine masses of Klingon ships. This direction changes the silhouette and
internal arrangement, not just the old ships' paint.

Build three rough studies: Shrike, a wide ambush raider with down-swept wings;
Vark, a long-neck cruiser with separated engine pods; and Gorath, a heavy warship
with a broad command head and thick armored wing roots. Keep the requested
sequence: floor plan, hull, then floor plan fitted to the hull. Retain interactive
3D deck cutaways and simple scale furnishings. Engine assets and tuning remain
outside this concept review.
