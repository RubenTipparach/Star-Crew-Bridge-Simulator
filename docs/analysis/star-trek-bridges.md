# Star Trek bridges: what the owner's references teach

The owner, 2026-10-05, sharing nine images of Star Trek bridges: "notice how elevation is
dynamic? the upper deck is for work, the lower one is walk way to move around, and the captains
chair is raised on a platform, with two consoles for helms and tactical a sub platform. notice
how majority of bridge consoles are buit into walls? notice the shape of the bridge, usualy this
eiter confirms to the bridge shape which is circular on star trek ships or the ship shape liket
he one found on the defiant", then "please document these carefully, make a few variations, and
I'll give you my feed back on whats the best".

This is that record. It describes each image the owner sent (the images belong to their artists
and publishers, so they are cited, not copied into the repository; CLAUDE.md section 15: take
the shape, not the art), then draws the lessons for the Tern's bridge. The variations are
`openspec/changes/bridge-stations` design section 11a and the mockup
`docs/mockups/bridge-variants.html`.

Numbers read off a drawing are estimates from its proportions, marked "about", using a standing
person of 1.8 m as the scale.

## The references

### R1. A circular first-series bridge, isometric cutaway (Ex Astris Scientia)

Ex Astris Scientia's cutaway of a circular starship bridge in the first television series'
style, with an inset labelling the turbolift, the captain, the helm and "Number One".

- **Plan:** a circle drawn as about sixteen flat wall segments, about 9 m across. The turbolift
  is a cylinder set into the aft wall, its door opening onto the room; a domed cap shows the
  shaft above.
- **Levels:** two. An **upper ring** about 1.5 m deep runs all round the wall. The **lower well**
  in the middle is about one step (about 0.3 m) down. A dark curved **railing** on posts follows
  the ring's inner edge, with **gaps** where steps lead down.
- **Consoles:** every station but two is **built into the wall**: a continuous sloped desk at
  sitting height, a band of buttons and lights, and screens set into the wall above, in bays
  between upright frames. Swivel chairs on pedestals stand on the ring, facing the wall.
- **Command:** in the well, the **captain's chair** sits on a small raised plinth at the centre.
  In front of it, nearer the viewscreen, a **single free-standing console** seats the helm and the
  navigator ("Number One") side by side, facing forward.
- **Circulation:** the turbolift lets out onto the ring; people walk the ring behind the wall
  stations and step down into the well through the railing's gaps.
- **Look:** white and pale blue, dark navy console faces, coloured indicator lights; the viewscreen
  is on the cut-away forward wall.

### R2. A bridge with a briefing room, isometric cutaway (Ex Astris Scientia, Gilso schematics)

An Ex Astris Scientia cutaway credited to "Gilso Star Trek Schematics", labelled: captain's
chair, elevator, science station, comm station, system monitor, conference table, to ready room,
engineering station, helm station, main viewscreen.

- **Plan:** a **round main room** about 11 m across with a **rectangular briefing room** attached
  on one side (a conference table, wall displays, the door to the ready room) and a **cylindrical
  elevator** between them at the back. The outer wall is thick and stepped: alcoves bulge out at
  the forward corners and at the viewscreen.
- **Levels:** three. An **upper ring** along the walls for the wall stations; a **middle floor**
  that holds the captain's chair on a **round raised platform** with an arc of railing behind it;
  and a **sunken front well** (darker floor, toward the viewscreen) holding the **helm**, a long
  curved desk with one chair facing forward.
- **Consoles:** science and communications are **built into the wall** in angled bays, each a
  sloped desk under a row of displays, with chairs at the desk. The **engineering station** is the
  exception: a **free-standing angled desk** at the platform's side, with its own chair.
- **Railings:** short curved rails at every level change: behind the captain, round the helm well,
  at the ring's edge.
- **Look:** warm grey and beige with blue-grey cut faces; screens framed in the walls.

### R3. A domed bridge, concept painting (trekmania.net)

A painted concept, published by trekmania.net and signed in its corner (the artist and the
production to verify).

- **Plan:** a round room under a **domed, ribbed ceiling** with a ring of light at its crown.
- **Levels:** an **oval well** bounded by a **curved railing on chrome posts**, the rail at about
  waist height; wall stations on the ring outside it.
- **Command:** a large **wraparound console** in the well seats the helm and others; the
  **captain's chair** is a white egg-shaped shell behind it; a small globe display beside it.
- **Consoles:** wall stations with **sloped panels under rows of display screens**, people seated
  at them facing the wall. A **rectangular viewscreen** high on the forward wall above them.
- **Light:** bright, even, white from above; a red turbolift door is the one saturated accent.

### R4. The same kind of bridge at red alert, concept painting

A second painting of a similar layout, lit red and green: red pools from the ceiling fixtures,
green walls, a figure in the open turbolift door, a curved railing across the foreground, a
spherical display at the side. It shows the room's **alert state carried by coloured light**, not
by changing the room.

### R5. A ship-shaped bridge, isometric cutaway (Ex Astris Scientia)

Ex Astris Scientia's cutaway of a small warship's bridge, labelled "CONN & OPS", right access,
left access, command chair.

- **Plan:** an **irregular polygon that follows the ship's prow**: angled walls meeting at the
  front, about 9 by 8 m. Two access doors at the back corners, left and right.
- **Levels:** one floor with a **raised oval platform** in the middle, its edge marked by a **red
  inlaid line and a blue band**.
- **Command:** the **command chair** on that platform, facing forward; **CONN and OPS** in front of
  it at a free-standing console with its own seats.
- **Consoles:** **all other stations are built into the walls**, each a desk with displays above in
  a recess, seats facing the walls, nobody more than a few steps from the captain.
- **Look:** pale grey walls, blue console bands, a warm floor; compact and purposeful.

### R6. The same warship, whole-ship cutaway

A cutaway of the whole ship whose bridge R5 shows. The bridge sits forward in the hull and **its
plan follows the hull's outline**: the bow's curve is the bridge's forward wall. Decks, the
engineering core and the compartments around it are packed into the same envelope. It is the
clearest picture of the owner's "ship shape" option.

### R7. A compact angular bridge, 3D render

A three-dimensional render (artist to verify).

- **Plan:** angular, with bays stepped in and out; a **round raised platform** at the back holding
  the captain's chair, and a briefing table beside it on the same level.
- **Levels:** platform, main floor, and a **curved helm console** in the middle with two chairs in
  front of it.
- **Floor:** **red and blue stripes inlaid in the floor follow the curve** of the helm console:
  circulation drawn on the deck.
- **Consoles:** **wall console banks** under rows of screens, separated by **upright pillars that
  carry light strips** (fixtures in the structure, our ribs).
- **Look:** dark grey, white light strips, one blue door at the back.

### R8. A large circular bridge with three command chairs, isometric cutaway

A cutaway of a large modern bridge (source to verify).

- **Plan:** round, with **two red turbolift doors** at the back and thick cut walls.
- **Levels:** an **outer ring** with wall consoles all round; a **raised rail station** behind the
  command area (a standing console along a curved rail); the **command area** lower, with **three
  chairs side by side** (captain in the middle, two officers flanking); **two free-standing
  consoles** in front of them facing the viewscreen.
- **Railings:** rails with light strips along each level change.
- **Look:** deep blue carpet, blue and grey consoles, red doors as the accent.

### R9. An angular split-level bridge, isometric cutaway (Ex Astris Scientia)

Ex Astris Scientia's cutaway labelled: communication station, to turbolift, captain's chair,
science station, to lower level, CONN, OPS (the ship to verify).

- **Plan:** an **irregular angular polygon**, about 10 by 9 m, its walls cut back into **alcoves**.
- **Levels:** **two, front and back**, split by a **two-step riser** running across the room. The
  **upper level at the back** holds the **captain's chair at the riser's edge**, the turbolift door,
  a door "to lower level" (a stair to the deck below), and **free-standing stand-up consoles** on
  pedestals at either side. The **lower level at the front** holds **CONN and OPS**: two reclined
  seats with consoles on angled pedestals, facing the viewscreen.
- **Consoles:** the communication and science stations are **built into wall alcoves**, framed by
  **structural fins pierced with triangular cutouts**, each a sloped desk under a wall of displays,
  two seats each.
- **Detail:** a wall-mounted systems grid, a light fixture over it, red accent stripes on the
  consoles, red doors.

## What they have in common

| Lesson | Where it shows | What it means for the Tern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Elevation is dynamic: two or three levels** | R1 ring and well; R2 ring, middle floor, sunken helm; R8 ring, rail station, command area; R9 front and back levels | The bridge is not one flat floor. A level per job: the wall stations on an **upper work level**, a **lower walkway** to move round on, the **captain raised on a platform**, the **helm and tactical on a sub-platform** in front. |
| **Consoles are built into the walls** | R1, R2, R3, R5, R7, R9 | Station consoles are **wall banks**: a sloped desk with displays set into the wall above, between upright frames, not desks standing in the room. Only the helm group (and the odd engineering or stand-up console, R2, R9) stands free. |
| **The shape is the bridge's or the ship's** | Circular: R1, R2, R3, R8. Ship-shaped: R5 and R6 | Either a **round room** (a ring of flat segments) inside the bow, or a room whose walls **follow the bow**, as the Tern's wedge does now. |
| **Railings mark every level change**, with gaps at the steps | R1, R2, R3, R4, R8 | A level change of a step or more gets a rail; the rail's gaps are where people go. |
| **The captain sees over the helm to the viewscreen** | All | Helm and tactical sit in front of and below the captain, both facing the viewscreen on the forward wall. |
| **Doors enter at the back** onto the upper level or the walkway | R1, R2, R5, R8, R9 | The aft door (the command passage) lets out behind the captain, never into a station's back. |
| **Frames carry the light and divide the consoles** | R7 light pillars; R9 pierced fins; R1 upright frames | Our generated ribs (`deck-pipeline` section 5a) become the console frames and carry light strips. |
| **Floor bands show the circulation** | R5 red line, R7 stripes | Inlaid stripes on the walkway and at platform edges. |
| **Alert is light, not geometry** | R4 | Already our rule: the three lighting states (CLAUDE.md section 9). |

## What the Tern's bridge does today, against them

The Tern's bridge (layout v2, 2026-10-05) is a **ship-shaped wedge** at the bow (R5's option),
3.5 m tall, with the viewscreen on its forward wall and windows in the raked walls either side.
But it is **one flat floor** with a 0.3 m dais for the captain, and its seven stations are
**desks standing in the room**: the four side stations face the raked walls from 1 m away
instead of being built into them. The references say: levels, wall-built consoles, rails.

The variations that apply these lessons are in `bridge-stations` design section 11a.
